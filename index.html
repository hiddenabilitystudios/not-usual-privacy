Privacy Policy

Not Your Usual Reminder. Effective 17 November 2026.

Not Your Usual Reminder is built to keep your tasks private. In short: we don't collect, store or share any of your data. Everything you type stays on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

Your tasks, the list of things you've done today, your reminders and your settings (such as sound on or off) are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. The app has no account, no sign-in and no server.

If you have turned on backup for your phone, Android may include the app's data in your own Google backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account; we cannot see or access it.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders and "time's up" alerts are scheduled by your phone's own notification system. The text of a reminder (your task) is shown in the notification and stays on your device.

Motion sensor

On the focus screen, the app reads your phone's motion sensor so the water on screen stays level when you tilt the phone. These readings are used instantly for the animation and are not stored or sent anywhere.

Analytics, ads and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Because your data lives only on your device, you can delete all of it at any time by clearing the app's data in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
