Privacy Policy

Not Your Usual Reminder, developed by Hidden Ability Studios. Effective 17 November 2026.

This policy explains how Not Your Usual Reminder ("the app") handles your information. The app is developed by Hidden Ability Studios ("we", "us"). In short: the app has no account and no server, and nothing you type into it is sent to us. The only personal information we receive is what you choose to send us by email.

What the app stores, and where

Your tasks, the list of things you've done today, your reminders and your settings (such as sound on or off) are saved on your device. The app never sends them to us or to anyone else. The app has no account, no sign-in and no server.

Android backup: if you have turned on backup for your phone, Android may copy the app's data (your tasks, reminders and settings) into your own Google account backup, so it can be restored on a new phone. Google handles that backup, it belongs to your account, and we cannot see or access it. You can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders and "time's up" alerts are scheduled by your phone's own notification system. The text of a reminder (your task) is shown in the notification and stays on your device.

Motion sensor

Only while the water timer is open on screen, the app reads your phone's accelerometer (its tilt) so the water stays level when you tilt the phone. Each reading is used instantly for the animation and is never saved or sent anywhere. The app stops reading the sensor when you close the timer, and does not use any other sensor.

Analytics, ads and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting tools and no tracking of any kind.

Android and Google Play diagnostics

Separately from the app, Android and Google Play may collect crash and performance information from your phone, depending on your device settings (for example, "Usage & diagnostics"). Google collects and handles this under its own privacy policy, not ours. Google may show us summary reports, such as how often the app crashes on a type of phone. These reports do not include your tasks or reminders, and we do not use them to identify you.

Who the app is for

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Deleting your data

Data on your phone: clearing the app's data in your phone's settings, or uninstalling the app, deletes everything the app stored on that phone.

Data in your Google backup: Deleting the app's local data or uninstalling it may leave existing Google backup copies. Management, deletion and restoration of those copies depend on the device's and Google account's backup settings. Available controls vary by device and Android version.
